About Printora

Printing that keeps up with your deadline.

Printora is a print shop in Cebu City for people who need good prints without a big bill. Students, teachers and small businesses bring us modules, research papers, posters and presentations, and we get them ready to hand in or hand out.

We also built PrintCheck, a quick look at your file before it hits the paper, so a wrong page size or a missing page gets caught early instead of after you have paid.

Three promises, one shop

Affordable

Clear per-page prices that start at ₱2, plus student rates for school requirements. You know the cost before you order.

Fast

Printing is built around deadlines. Send your details early and we work to have your prints ready when you need them.

Quality

Clean black and white text, vivid color, and neat binding, checked before it leaves the counter.
